## What it shows about Sackmann's approach (inference from the list)

- **Format:** ~12.5 minutes, follow-along video, a flow that goes standing warm-up → floor (core,
  breathing, hips) → back up through lunges and squats. Many moves, short doses (5–25 reps or 5–10 s),
  not a few long holds. Day-numbered, so it is a daily habit program.
- **Coverage:** feet/ankles (pogos, raises, rolls), core and breathing (dead bug series, rock back
  breathing), hip rotation (90/90), hip extension (heel bridge), T-spine (spider man, Y's), shoulders
  (arm swings, yoga push-ups, lat stretch), wrists (palm stretch).

## Conflicts with the user's hip (data/app_inventory.md; src/lib/hip_protocol.js)

The hip flare started after a pull in a 90/90 stretch; sitting, lunges and forward-driving hip-flexor
stretches aggravate it. In this Day 10 list these hit the flagged positions:
- 90/90 Hold and 90/90 Transitions (the move that triggered the flare)
- Spider Man Push Downs / Push Outs / T-Spine (deep lunge, hip-flexor and rotation end range)
- Lunge × Reach (forward-driving hip-flexor lunge stretch)
- Alternating Front Lunges and Side Lunges (lunges)
- Deep Squat × Y's × Toe Touches (deep hip flexion)

Hip-friendly items that can stay as written: pogos (if pain-free), arm swings, marches, ankle/calf
raises, ankle rolls, jumping jacks, sit-down roll ups, the dead bug series, single-leg heel bridge,
rock back breathing (keep it short of the pinch), sit-down lat stretch, 3-way palm stretch,
yoga push-ups.
