# Portia Mitchell (FramedFIT) and similar mobility coaches — review

Researched 2026-11-14 at the user's request ("her and others train professional athletes for mobility").
**Nothing here is in the app yet.** Approve moves below, then they go in as catalog exercises (tagged,
so the generator, Swap lists and warm-up builder can use them) with the reel as the video.

## Who she is

- **Portia Mitchell**, founder of **FramedFIT**, Nashville. NASM-certified. Former competitive cheerleader
  and track athlete. Her method is "Strength and Stretch" (mind-to-muscle control), sold online as
  *The Mobility Method*. Sources: [itsframedfit.com](https://itsframedfit.com/),
  [Nashville Voyager interview](https://nashvillevoyager.com/interview/hidden-gems-meet-portia-mitchell-of-framedfit/).
- Her Facebook ([portia.mitchell.75/reels](https://www.facebook.com/portia.mitchell.75/reels/), 51K followers,
  81 reels) shows NFL players (one reel is captioned "my NFL athlete"), Vanderbilt linemen, and high-school
  team sessions. No independent source names her pro clients.
- **Her style:** active range, not passive stretching. Seated bench hip work, half-kneeling hip work on
  a step, single-leg control on a BOSU, adductor loading for groin health, ankles. It is very close to
  Vernon Griffith's (already a provider here), so it fits the hip plan.

## How they go into the knowledge layer

- New moves via `lib.ensure_exercise(name, source, tags={...})` with taxonomy tags (`docs/EXERCISE_TAXONOMY.md`):
  pattern, role, muscles, equipment, level, `hip_focus`, `progresses_by: variation`.
- Source tags: `framedfit` (Portia), `frc`, `atg`. Creator credited in the note, as with Griffith.
- `python3 data/tag_exercises.py` → `node data/check_catalog.mjs` → generator `--sanity`.
- They join warm-up pools and Swap lists (same pattern/level neighbours) automatically once tagged.
  Nothing replaces a plan's own moves unless you ask.
