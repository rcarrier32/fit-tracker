# Daily mobility routines of established mobility/joint-health systems (FRC/Kinstretch, ATG, Prehab Guys, Squat U, Starrett, apps, shoulder basics)

Method note: WebFetch was blocked for every primary site tried (boostcamp.app, equinox.com, atgonlinecoaching.com, thereadystate.com, kneesovertoesguy.substack.com, theprehabguys.com). All findings below come from search-result snippets/summaries of the cited URLs, not full-page reads. Many doses are from third-party write-ups, flagged as such. No primary Spina/FRC dosing document was found.

## Q1. Which joints and movements appear in nearly every daily routine? What is the consensus minimal set?

### Takeaway
Across systems the recurring daily targets are: ankle dorsiflexion (knee-over-toe), knee flexion under load, hip rotation + hip extension (couch stretch), spine (flexion/segmentation), and shoulder (overhead flexion + scapular control/external rotation). A defensible minimal set for a basketball player is: hip CARs + shoulder CARs, tib raise, backward walking or Patrick step, ATG split squat (or knee-to-wall ankle rocks), couch stretch, and a scap/rotator-cuff drill (pull-apart or band ER), with floor sitting/deep squat accumulated through the day.

### Cited Findings
**FRC / Kinstretch (CARs)**
- A Kinstretch-instructor low-intensity "Morning CARs" follow-along (~14 min) covers neck, spine, shoulders, scapula, elbows, wrists, spinal segmentation, hips, knees, ankles and toes; described as good to do first thing after waking — [Kinstretch with Beard](https://kinstretchwithbeard.vhx.tv/videos/follow-along-morning-cars-routine)
- Same source: an 8-minute standing full-body morning CARs class also exists; doing the normal (higher-tension) CARs routine sometime in the day is still worthwhile, as it is the stronger training stimulus — [Kinstretch with Beard](https://kinstretchwithbeard.vhx.tv/no-equipment-classes/videos/follow-along-morning-cars-routine)
- Kinstretch-affiliated material frames a 10-15 min daily CARs routine as "maintenance plus information": every joint visits its current limit, and you get a daily read on where that limit sits — [Move With Purpose: FRC routine](https://www.movewithpurpose.com/frc-routine); [Move With Purpose: CARs Foundations](https://www.movewithpurpose.com/kinstretch-online-cars-foundations)
- A "joint health routine for parents over 40" newsletter suggests 2-3 reps per side for each of ten joints every morning — [timoj108 Substack](https://timoj108.substack.com/p/the-best-joint-health-routine-for) (non-FRC author)

**Knees Over Toes / ATG (Ben Patrick)**
- A Patrick TikTok routine lists (in order): floss up to 60 s; backward walking up to 5 min; seated calf raise 2 x ≥20; tibialis raise up to 20; calf & tib rotations 10/side; reverse step-up 2 x up to 20/side; bridge/Nordic/ham curl 2 x 5-15; ATG split squat 3 x up to 12/side; staggered RDL 3 x up to 12/side — [kneesovertoesguy TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@kneesovertoesguy/video/7380743577401412906)
- Coaching summary: his progressions start with backward walk, tibialis raise, ATG split squat, slant-board squat, Nordic curl — "starting easy and scalable" — [Coachway](https://coachway.io/articles/ben-patrick-bulletproof-knees-for-coaches/)
- BarBend lists 7 Patrick exercises for knee-injury prevention — [BarBend](https://barbend.com/kneesovertoesguy-7-exercises-to-help-prevent-knee-injuries/) (contents not read)
- ATG has a "Hoop Dreams Strength & Mobility System" aimed at basketball — [ATG Online Coaching](https://www.atgonlinecoaching.com/articles/article-hoop-dreams-strength-and-mobility-system) (page blocked; content unknown)

**Kelly Starrett / Built to Move (The Ready State)**
- Built to Move is organized around 10 "vital signs"; each assessment is followed by practices (hip/shoulder mobilizations, squatting variations, ways to add steps) — [Equinox](https://equinox.com/articles/2023/04/10-signs-that-you-are-built-to-move); [Ready State podcast](https://thereadystate.com/trs_podcast/built-to-move-vital-signs/)
- Vital-sign tests confirmed in search snippets: sit-and-rise test (hip ROM; a 2014 Eur J Prev Cardiol study linked inability to get up/down off the floor to higher mortality), breath-hold test, "Airport Scanner" arms-raise (shoulder flexion), "Old Man" balance test (single-leg balance, reach for a sock), couch test (hip extension) — [mindbodydad](https://www.mindbodydad.com/body/built-to-move-vital-signs); [Kinesophy review](https://kinesophy.com/vital-signs-for-physical-mobility-review-of-built-to-move-by-kelly-and-juliet-starrett/); [Versus Pilates review](https://versuspilates.com/reviews/built-to-move/)
- Practices: accumulate ≥30 min floor sitting per day (build up if daunting; kneeling and squatting count as variety) — [Deseret News](https://www.deseret.com/2023/10/17/23921547/why-you-should-sit-on-the-floor-daily-built-to-move/); [InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/wellness/sitting-on-floor-best-way-to-sit)
- Steps: track 3 days, then aim ≥8,000 steps/day — [Four Minute Books](https://fourminutebooks.com/built-to-move-summary/)

**Squat University (Horschig)**
- Ankle screen: half-kneeling knee-to-wall with big toe 5 inches from wall, heel down; pass = knee touches wall — [Squat University, Ankle Mobility Pt 1](https://squatuniversity.com/2015/11/05/the-squat-fix-ankle-mobility-pt-1/); Mike Reinold uses the same 5" pass/fail — [Mike Reinold](https://mikereinold.com/ankle-mobility-exercises-to-improve-dorsiflexion/)
- Squat U reply: 5" is a relative distance shown in research to catch significant deficits (not perfectly scaled to foot size) — [Squat U comments](https://squatuniversity.com/2015/11/05/the-squat-fix-ankle-mobility-pt-1/comment-page-1/). Conflict: a clinical handout uses <5 cm as "restricted" — [Carepatron](https://www.carepatron.com/files/knee-to-wall-test.pdf)
- Horschig's fix sequence: mobilize, foam roll, stretch (ankle) and similar 4-step hip sequence ending in posterior-chain activation — [Squat U Ankle Pt 3](https://squatuniversity.com/2015/11/19/the-squat-fix-ankle-mobility-pt-3/); [Squat U Hip Pt 3](https://squatuniversity.com/2015/12/28/the-squat-fix-hip-mobility-pt-3/)
- Squat U Club warm-up includes bent-knee ankle rocks, hip windshield wipers, ~1 min/side foam rolling — [TrainHeroic Squat U Club](https://marketplace.trainheroic.com/workout-plan/team/squat-u-club)

**Prehab Guys**
- Basketball Prehab Warm Up is a 5-phase routine — [Prehab Guys](https://theprehabguys.com/blog/basketball-prehab-warm-up/) (page blocked; phases not read)
- Their full-body mobility program is 8 weeks at 2-4x/week (not daily) — [Prehab Guys library](https://library.theprehabguys.com/program-full-body-mobility/)
- Shoulder sequencing for basketball: establish scapular control first, then open-chain ER; suggested drills include prone T's/W's and swimmers — [Prehab Guys basketball shoulder instability](https://theprehabguys.com/basketball-shoulder-instability-rehab/)
- Overhead-athlete prevention program (IR/ER-focused) cites a study with 28% lower risk of shoulder problems and 22% lower risk of substantial problems vs routine warm-up — [Prehab Guys shoulder prevention](https://theprehabguys.com/shoulder-injury-prevention-program/)

**Shoulder basics (general sources)**
- Banded face pulls: PT-recommended for posterior cuff (ER at end of pull) 3-4x/week — [Fit&Well](https://www.fitandwell.com/exercise/strength-workouts/this-is-the-one-move-a-physical-therapist-recommends-for-people-who-sit-a-lot-and-suffer-from-shoulder-pain)
- Band ER at side "most commonly prescribed rotator cuff exercise", 3 x 15; pull-aparts + ER 2 x 15-20 as warm-up — [Benchmark PT](https://www.benchmarkpt.com/blog/shoulder-resistance-band-exercises-for-strength-injury-prevention/); [Resport Chicago](https://resportchicago.com/resources/physical-therapy/the-3-most-effective-banded-shoulder-exercises/)
- Scap push-ups: quadruped/knees-down regression, full push-up position progression — [Yahoo Sports basketball prehab](https://sports.yahoo.com/basketball-prehab-exercises-4-trouble-113613960.html)

### Inferences
- Every system hits the ankle (dorsiflexion) and hip; Starrett and FRC add overhead shoulder and spine; ATG adds tibialis and loaded knee flexion. For a basketball player (landing, cutting, overhead), ankle + knee + hip + shoulder are non-negotiable.
- The overlap suggests a 5-7 item daily set: (1) hip CARs, (2) shoulder/scap CARs, (3) tib raise, (4) backward walk or Patrick step, (5) ATG split squat or knee-to-wall ankle rocks, (6) couch stretch, (7) band pull-apart/ER; plus "ambient" floor sitting/deep-squat time and steps outside the routine.

### Gaps
- No primary FRC/Spina dose; no Prehab Guys daily minimal routine (site blocked); Built to Move's full list of 10 vital signs and exact per-sign practices not confirmed (search only surfaced ~5); ATG Hoop Dreams contents unknown.

## Q2. What doses and frequencies? How do they progress?

### Takeaway
Mobility drills: 2-5 slow reps per direction (CARs), 30 s-2 min holds/positions, total 5-15 min daily. ATG strength-mobility drills: 15-25 reps for tib/Patrick step, 5x5 to 3x12 for split squat, 2-5 min backward walking. Progress by range first, then reps, then load or elevation, only when pain-free.

### Cited Findings
**CARs**
- Hip CARs: ≥3 rotations per direction per hip daily, more as capacity builds; keep pain ≤3/10 — [Chiropractor Toronto](https://www.chiropractortoronto.com/monthly-mobility-exercise-hip-cars/); other site: 5 each direction, each leg, 2 sets — [Posture Guy Mike](https://postureguymike.com/exercises/hip-cars)
- Shoulder CARs: 1-2 slow reps per side daily enough to "check in" — [Yoga Framework](https://yogaframework.com/shoulder-controlled-articular-rotation/)
- General: 2-3 reps recommended; consistency matters more than the number — [Perfect Stride PT](https://perfectstridept.com/blogs/controlled-articular-rotations-cars/)
- FRC tension levels (0/50/100% "air resistance", not load); daily, within pain-free available motion — [MVMT](https://www.mvmtgb.com/blog/controlled-articular-rotations-cars). Rehab use: small pain-free range, ~10% tension; warm-up: larger ranges, more tension — [Perfect Stride PT](https://perfectstridept.com/blogs/controlled-articular-rotations-cars/)
- Progression in FRC is by increasing irradiation/tension and range, not added weight — [MVMT](https://www.mvmtgb.com/blog/controlled-articular-rotations-cars)

**ATG doses**
- Tibialis raise: 2 x up to 15 (Knee Ability program) — [Patrick Substack Knee Ability 3/10](https://kneesovertoesguy.substack.com/p/atg-knee-ability-series-3-of-10); up to 20 (TikTok) — [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@kneesovertoesguy/video/7380743577401412906); 2 x 15-25 (third-party) — [Coachway](https://coachway.io/articles/ben-patrick-bulletproof-knees-for-coaches/); [Wellbeing Connection](https://www.wellbeingconnection.life/blog/daily-knee-reset-58-minute-gentle-rehab-atg-inspired)
- Backward walking: up to 5 min (Patrick); 2-5 min daily or up a small incline if no sled (third-party) — [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@kneesovertoesguy/video/7380743577401412906); [Wellbeing Connection](https://www.wellbeingconnection.life/blog/daily-knee-reset-58-minute-gentle-rehab-atg-inspired); build to 5-10 min backward treadmill — [Set For Set](https://www.setforset.com/blogs/news/joe-rogan-kneesovertoesguy-ben-patrick-how-to-fix-knee-pain)
- Couch stretch: "Couch Stretch Pulse 2 x 20" or "2 x 30 s" in Patrick programs (per search summary) — [Patrick Substack Knee Ability 3/10](https://kneesovertoesguy.substack.com/p/atg-knee-ability-series-3-of-10)
- Patrick step: beginner standard 25 consecutive reps to full ankle bend on flat ground; loaded/elevated 2-4 x 15-30 — [A1 Athlete Patrick step](https://a1athlete.com/patrick-step/); [A1 Athlete ATG standards](https://a1athlete.com/atg-standards/)
- ATG split squat: ATG Zero week 1 5x5 each leg; Patrick's own training 3 x up to 12/side — [Medium ATG Zero log](https://medium.com/@dreamchaserelite/atg-zero-workout-for-this-week-3140ae7fe461); [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@kneesovertoesguy/video/7380743577401412906)
- Elephant walk → Jefferson curl ladder (coach, not Patrick): high elephant walk 3x25/leg → low elephant walk 3x25/leg → BW Jefferson curl 5x5 → weighted 5x5; spinal flexion ≥1x/week — [movementgems TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@movementgems/video/7307460120127098154). ATG Jefferson curl standard: 10 reps at 25% BW, wrists below toes; start 0-5 kg — [A1 Athlete ATG standards](https://a1athlete.com/atg-standards/)
- L-sit: ATG standard 15 s on flat ground; ATG Zero plan 2 x 1 min (community plan, likely includes elevated regression) — [A1 Athlete](https://a1athlete.com/knees-over-toes-guy-exercises/); [Medium ATG Zero](https://medium.com/@dreamchaserelite/atg-zero-workout-for-this-week-3140ae7fe461)
- ATG Zero program structure: 12 weeks, 3 days/week; unilateral sets done on both sides; Zero → Basics → Pro progression — [Boostcamp ATG Zero](https://www.boostcamp.app/users/atg-zero-knees-over-toes)

**Starrett / Squat U**
- Built to Move activities total "no more than 10 minutes per day"; Equinox: minimum ~10 min/day of mobilizations — [Versus Pilates](https://versuspilates.com/reviews/built-to-move/); [Equinox](https://equinox.com/articles/2023/04/10-signs-that-you-are-built-to-move)
- Starrett's own nightly "10-10-10": 10 pull-ups, 10 push-ups, 10 squats at 10 pm — reported in search summary of [Equinox](https://equinox.com/articles/2023/04/10-signs-that-you-are-built-to-move) (unverified)
- Squat U: at least 2 minutes on each restricted area — [Squat U Ankle Pt 3](https://squatuniversity.com/2015/11/19/the-squat-fix-ankle-mobility-pt-3/); [Squat U Hip Pt 3](https://squatuniversity.com/2015/12/28/the-squat-fix-hip-mobility-pt-3/)
- Non-Squat-U: aim ~5 min total deep-squat time per day — [LiftStrong](https://www.liftstrong.com/articles/mobility-routine-for-lifters-15-minutes-to-move-better)

**Tendon isometrics (Prehab Guys)**
- Patellar/Achilles isometrics: 5 x 45 s holds at ~70% max, 2 min rest — [Prehab Guys jumper's knee](https://theprehabguys.com/jumpers-knee-exercises/); [Prehab Guys Achilles](https://theprehabguys.com/blog/rehab-achilles-tendinopathy/)
- Isometrics can be daily (1-2x/day, 3-5 x 30-45 s) and as a warm-up; heavy slow resistance limited to 2-3x/week per tendon region — [E3 Rehab](https://e3rehab.com/how-to-rehab-tendon-injuries-and-pain/); [The Workout Mag](https://theworkoutmag.com/training/how-to-strengthen-tendons-evidence-based)

**Shoulder doses**
- Band ER 3 x 15; pull-aparts/ER 2 x 15-20 pre-upper-body; rehab 10-15 reps 2-3x daily per PT — [Benchmark PT](https://www.benchmarkpt.com/blog/shoulder-resistance-band-exercises-for-strength-injury-prevention/); [Taiclet Bands](https://taicletbands.com/blog/resistance-band-shoulder-exercises-guide/)
- Dead hang: 60-90 s total/day (split) or 60-180 s; start 5-20 s, add 5 s — [GMB](https://gmb.io/hanging/); [The Workout Mag](https://theworkoutmag.com/supplements/hanging-from-bar-benefits-evidence-guide). Conflict: one source warns daily hanging adds tendon overload risk, suggests 10-15 s 3x/week to start — [Gripp](https://www.gripp.fit/blog/dead-hangs-for-shoulder-pain-what-the-research-actually-says)

**Apps (routine length / personalization)**
- GOWOD: ~15-min, 10-movement self-assessment scoring each body area; daily routines in 8, 15 or 22 min; 3-min option; sport-specific programming from assessment — [Mytour](https://mytour.vn/en/blog/lifestyle/can-an-app-actually-improve-your-mobility-mytour.html); [Enjoy the Pain](https://enjoythepain.net/pliability-vs-gowod/); [Feeling Better](https://feelingbetter.io/pliability-review/)
- Pliability (ex-ROMWOD): ~3-min assessment; most routines 12-20 min; sessions offered in shorter (~15) and longer (~25) versions; one comparison says 10-60 min — [Pliability](https://pliability.com/stories/best-flexibility-apps); [Feeling Better](https://feelingbetter.io/pliability-review/); [Fitness AI Trends](https://fitnessaitrends.com/blog/gowod-vs-pliability-2026/)
- Bend: 5- or 10-min routines, personalized by experience, focus area and goals; does not measure mobility — [Pliability list](https://pliability.com/stories/best-flexibility-apps); [Limber](https://limberbody.app/articles/comparisons/apps-that-measure-flexibility)
- Caveat: these are affiliate/competitor reviews; GOWOD assessment is self-reported — [Fitness AI Trends](https://fitnessaitrends.com/blog/gowod-vs-pliability-2026/)

### Inferences
- Time budget convergence: apps (5-22 min, most popular 10-15), Starrett (≤10 min), Kinstretch morning CARs (8-15 min), ATG-style daily knee reset (5-8 min). A 10-12 min daily routine is the mainstream sweet spot; 5-7 exercises at ~90 s each fits.
- Two dosing families should be kept distinct in the app: (a) mobility/CARs: reps per direction, slow, low-moderate tension, daily; (b) ATG strength-mobility: reps-to-standard (e.g. tib raise to 25, Patrick step to 25), progressed via range → reps → load/incline.
- Personalization in apps = an initial assessment (scored body areas) feeding focus-area selection plus selectable session length. An in-app analogue: knee-to-wall 5", couch test, overhead arms test, sit-and-rise, single-leg balance as periodic re-tests.

### Gaps
- Official ATG Zero sets/reps table not verified (Boostcamp blocked; Medium log is community-made). No primary Prehab Guys or Kinstretch daily dosing. Starrett "10-10-10" anecdote not confirmed by a primary source.

## Q3. How do they differ for a ~40-year-old vs younger athletes?

### Takeaway
None of the systems publishes a distinct 40+ protocol. The age-specific advice is qualitative: start at lower-load regressions (backward sled/walking, elevated or partial ranges), consistency over intensity, pain-guided progression, and longer time at each step.

### Cited Findings
- Patrick: "old people do backwards sled"; beginners lean the sled back, avoiding knee/foot load; backward sled is an entry point for people who think their knees are "shot" — [Just Fly Sports podcast](https://www.just-fly-sports.com/podcast-148-ben-patrick/); [Set For Set](https://www.setforset.com/blogs/news/joe-rogan-kneesovertoesguy-ben-patrick-how-to-fix-knee-pain)
- Aging athletes "can reverse decline through progressive overload, smart regressions, and consistency over intensity" (Tim Ferriss #835 summary) — [Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-tim-ferriss-show-2025-11-12-episode-summary-835-ben-patrick-kneesovertoesguy-20-minute-workouts-that-produce-wild-results-from-chronic-knee-pain-to-dunking-basketballs-lessons-from-charles-poliquin-bulletproofing-the-lower-body-and-more)
- Caveat: KOT exercises may not suit everyone; consider biomechanics/injury history; 40+ with knee issues should consult a clinician — [Set For Set](https://www.setforset.com/blogs/news/joe-rogan-kneesovertoesguy-ben-patrick-how-to-fix-knee-pain)
- Built to Move is aimed at general adults/longevity; sit-and-rise linked to mortality risk in middle-aged/older adults — [mindbodydad](https://www.mindbodydad.com/body/built-to-move-vital-signs)
- Elephant walk → Jefferson curl ladder is explicitly for the "fragile" (start lower; spinal flexion 1-2x/week) — [movementgems TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@movementgems/video/7307460120127098154)
- FRC: frequency/intensity should depend on training background, recovery capacity and current joint health — [Move With Purpose](https://www.movewithpurpose.com/frc-routine)
- Prehab Guys shoulder prehab is for those with 0-4/10 pain (mild/no pain) — [Prehab Guys Shoulder Prehab](https://theprehabguys.com/shoulder-prehab-program/)

### Inferences
- For a 40-year-old: keep daily items low-fatigue (CARs, tib raise, backward walk, couch stretch, band work), and put heavier/loaded items (weighted Jefferson curl, loaded split squat, Nordic) on 2-3x/week training days rather than daily.
- Use a pain ceiling (≤3/10, per CARs guidance) as the progression gate.

### Gaps
- No source found giving age-adjusted doses (e.g. more reps, longer holds) for 40+ in any of these systems.

## Q4. Daily vs pre-activity vs post-activity?

### Takeaway
Systems treat a low-intensity daily pass (morning CARs / ≤10 min Starrett / ATG basics) as maintenance and "joint check-in", and a separate, more targeted/higher-tension version as warm-up. Heavier loading and longer-hold stretching are not daily for most.

### Cited Findings
- Morning low-intensity CARs on waking; higher-intensity normal CARs elsewhere in day is the stronger stimulus — [Kinstretch with Beard](https://kinstretchwithbeard.vhx.tv/videos/follow-along-morning-cars-routine)
- Pre-workout: CARs for target joint only, slower and with more tension than the morning pass — [Move With Purpose](https://www.movewithpurpose.com/frc-routine); warm-up use builds awareness/tension before workouts — [Kinstretch: personal practice](https://kinstretch.com/building-personal-kinstretch-practice/)
- Rationale offered for daily joint movement (commercial claim): full-range motion maintains synovial membrane/fluid — [Movement Guild](https://movementguild.com/services/kinstretch/) (not primary research)
- Tendon isometrics: daily or as warm-up, 1-2x/day — [E3 Rehab](https://e3rehab.com/how-to-rehab-tendon-injuries-and-pain/)
- Pull-aparts/ER as pre-upper-body warm-up; face pulls 3-4x/week — [Benchmark PT](https://www.benchmarkpt.com/blog/shoulder-resistance-band-exercises-for-strength-injury-prevention/); [Fit&Well](https://www.fitandwell.com/exercise/strength-workouts/this-is-the-one-move-a-physical-therapist-recommends-for-people-who-sit-a-lot-and-suffer-from-shoulder-pain)
- Prehab Guys: shoulder prevention program done as warm-up reduced shoulder problems vs routine warm-up — [Prehab Guys](https://theprehabguys.com/shoulder-injury-prevention-program/); also a basketball-specific 5-phase pre-activity warm-up exists — [Prehab Guys](https://theprehabguys.com/blog/basketball-prehab-warm-up/)
- ATG Zero itself is 3 days/week, not daily; Prehab Guys full-body mobility 2-4x/week — [Boostcamp](https://www.boostcamp.app/users/atg-zero-knees-over-toes); [Prehab Guys library](https://library.theprehabguys.com/program-full-body-mobility/)
- Squat U uses mobility pre-squat with test-retest (do 5" test, mobilize, retest squat) — [Squat U Ankle Pt 1](https://squatuniversity.com/2015/11/05/the-squat-fix-ankle-mobility-pt-1/)

### Inferences
- App design: a daily 10-min "maintenance" routine (CARs + ATG basics + band shoulder) that doubles as the pre-practice warm-up with higher tension/added jumps; long static holds (couch stretch 2 min) better post-session or evening.
- Test-retest (Squat U style) is a cheap built-in progress metric.

### Gaps
- No primary source on post-activity specifics from these systems; static-stretching-before-play evidence was outside this search and not covered here.
