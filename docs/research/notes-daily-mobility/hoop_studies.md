## 1. What programs does HoopStudy sell, and what do public descriptions say they contain?

### Takeaway
Most HoopStudy products are skills courses. Only three touch mobility or strength:
- **Hoop Mobility**: a 7-day Playbook program of about 10-15 min/day.
- **Ten Minute Routine**: 10 minutes a day for 10 days, on daily movement and "compounding strength and mobility".
- A **Basketball Strength Program**: sold in a 2022 bundle and part of the 20-day "Player Transformation".

Their exercise contents are not public.

## 2. Which exercises does DJ Sackmann repeatedly recommend for joint health and mobility? Doses and frequency?

### Takeaway
No public source names specific joint-health exercises with doses from Sackmann. The only stated dose is product-level: about 10-15 minutes every day for Hoop Mobility, and 10 minutes a day for 10 days for the Ten Minute Routine.

### Cited Findings
- **Hoop Mobility dose.** About 10-15 minutes daily over a 7-day program, aimed at "muscular and joint health". — [Playbook: Hoop Mobility](https://my.playbookapp.io/dj-sackmann/programs/hoop-mobility/627)
- **Ten Minute Routine dose.** 10 minutes a day for 10 days, combining strength and mobility. — [HoopStudy courses](https://courses.hoopstudy.com/)
- **"Dribble and Stretch with DJ Sackmann"** (YouTube, HoopStudy, about 6+ years old). He walks through "a five part series you can do to stretch while stationary dribbling". The individual stretches were not visible in the search result. — [YouTube: Dribble and Stretch with DJ Sackmann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SzA54-esGE8)
- **No social posts found.** Searches for his Instagram, TikTok or X posts on hip, ankle, knee, groin, T-spine or shoulder mobility found none. Results were generic content from other coaches (for example Hooper's Beta and Hoops King), which should not be credited to Sackmann. — [search results incl. Hooper's Beta ankle routine](https://www.hoopersbeta.com/library/increase-ankle-mobility-amp-strength-for-massive-footwork-gains)

### Inferences
- "Dribble and Stretch" is an old video, but it suggests Sackmann likes to **combine** mobility with ball work. One practical idea for a time-limited player-coach: do static or held positions (deep squat, lunge, 90/90 hip position) while pound-dribbling. This is an inference, not a stated prescription.
- For ankle, knee, hip and T-spine specifics, the final report will have to rely on the other sources (Knees Over Toes, FRC, Prehab Guys, PJF and the research evidence). HoopStudy offers no documented exercise-level guidance here.

### Gaps
- Which joints Hoop Mobility targets and which movements it uses (for example ankle dorsiflexion, 90/90, couch stretch, T-spine rotation).
- Whether the routines use isometrics, loaded end-range work, or passive stretching.

## 3. Warm-up structure before court sessions, daily vs weekly items, and philosophy

### Takeaway
His public warm-up content is **skill-based** (finishing and ball-handling "prep" routines), not a documented physical warm-up. His stated philosophy is about consistency, habit and planning the week, not about joint-specific methods.

### Cited Findings
- **Pre-game/practice routine.** On X he posted a "Pre Game/Practice Handle Routine". He says it "Builds focus & confidence", "Establishes consistency" and "Prepares your mind & body to perform at your best", and that "Elite players don't just show up, they prepare with purpose." — [DJ Sackmann on X](https://x.com/DJSackmann/status/1878837426837147878)
- **Floater warm-up.** A Dr. Dish blog post presents a "2 foot floater series" warm-up from Sackmann, to get moving and build footwork and touch near the rim. — [Dr. Dish: Floater Warm-Up with DJ Sackmann](https://blog.drdishbasketball.com/basketball-drills-floater-warm-up-with-dj-sackmann)
- **Weekly planning.** On X: "The workout is only part of it. How you structure your week matters. Set specific goals. Know your time, location, and activity. Then build your week around them." A full video is on the HoopStudy YouTube channel. — [DJ Sackmann on X](https://x.com/DJSackmann/status/2097725193900752930)
- **Morning routine.** In an interview, he calls his morning routine "very important" because he is on the road a third of the year and needs a consistent routine for his internal clock. — [Authority Magazine interview](https://medium.com/authority-magazine/how-dj-sackmann-thrives-as-an-athlete-turned-entrepreneur-521afa053d01)
- **Habit framing.** The mobility product copy centers on "habit and accountability of prioritizing mobility". — [Playbook: Hoop Mobility](https://my.playbookapp.io/dj-sackmann/programs/hoop-mobility/627)

### Inferences
- The routine design can borrow two HoopStudy principles:
  - **Fixed time, fixed place, every day.** This matches the habit framing and the weekly-planning post.
  - **Follow the physical warm-up with a short ball-skill "prep" block** (handle routine or 2-foot floaters) before court sessions.
- The physical part of the warm-up (raising body temperature, dynamic mobility, landing and jumping prep) should come from the other sources, since nothing documented here covers it.

### Gaps
- No documented Sackmann stance on static vs dynamic stretching, isometrics, plyometric or tendon loading, or movement screens. Nothing was found in searches of podcasts, X or interviews. A podcast search returned no Sackmann episodes.
