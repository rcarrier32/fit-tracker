## Week structure (7-day plan, repeated 8 weeks)
Day 1 Strength 1 · Day 2 Sports Performance 1 · Day 3 Strength 2 · Day 4 Sports Specific 2 · Day 5 Strength 3 ·
Day 6 Recovery · Day 7 Recovery. Two recovery days a week are programmed.

## Training warm-up
Tissue work: LAX/soft ball on calves, hamstrings, glutes — 1 round of 30 s per muscle group. ("Pregame Warm Up
Routine" is named but not written out.)

## Strength days (all exercises 8 reps; sets by phase)
- Day 1: Dumbbell Bench · Single Arm DB Row · Dumbbell Reverse Lunge · Hollow Hold Sit Up
- Day 3: Dumbbell Goblet Squat · DB Cossack Lunge · Yoga Push Up with Heel Touch · DB Glute Bridge · Prone Pull Through
- Day 5: DB ½ Kneeling Clean and Press · Bent Over DB Row · DB 2pt RDL · Single Leg DB Heel Bridge · Single Leg Wall Stride
- Volume (high school): weeks 1-2 3×8, weeks 3-8 4×8 (middle school: 2×8 → 3×8 → 4×8 in weeks 7-8).
- Progression: record weights every exercise; work up to a top set (e.g. 10, 15, 20, 25 lb); when restarting in
  weeks 5-6, start from the top set hit in weeks 1-2.

## Sports performance days (Day 2 / Day 4)
4-Way Extensive Ankle Pop Series 2×10 · OH March 2×10 · A-March 2×10 · A-Skip 2×10 · Power Skip 2×5 ·
Falling Sprint warm-ups (10 yd at 50/75/100%) · then Day 2: 90/90 Sprints (4→10 × 10 yd at 100% over the weeks),
90/90 Single Power Jumps (3×3 → 5×3), Lateral Bounders (3×3 → 5×3); Day 4: Falling Sprints (4→10 × 10 yd),
Paused Squat Jumps (3×3 → 5×3). "Have someone time you and record time."

## RECOVERY days (Day 6 and Day 7 — identical) — the part that matters for daily joint health
1. Foam Roll and LAX Ball series: calves, hamstrings, glutes — 1 round of 30 s each muscle group
2. Hook Plank — 2 rounds of 10 breaths
3. 90/90 Lift with Internal Rotation — 2 rounds of 10 breaths
4. 90/90 Single Leg Bridge — 2 rounds of 10 reps each
5. 90/90 Knee Rolls — 2 rounds of 10 reps each side
6. Single Leg ISO Hold — 2 rounds of 25 s
7. Single Leg ISO Heel Bridge Hold — 2 rounds of 25 s

About 10-12 minutes. Mostly lying on the back, breathing-led, with isometric holds.

## Important: this "90/90" is NOT the seated 90/90 stretch that set off the hip flare
In Menner's recovery list, "90/90 Lift", "90/90 Single Leg Bridge" and "90/90 Knee Rolls" are, by their names
and structure, the Postural Restoration Institute (PRI) style **supine 90/90 position** — lying on the back, hips
and knees bent to 90° with the feet on a wall or box, breathing with a slight posterior pelvic tilt and
hamstring/glute engagement. (Inference from the names, doses "10 breaths" and the bridge/knee-roll pairings;
the PDF has no pictures of these moves in the extracted text, so confirm against the video/workbook images.)
That is the opposite end of the spectrum from the seated 90/90 hip-rotation stretch flagged in the app's hip notes:
it unloads the hip, matches the Hip tab's "Posterior Pelvic Tilt Breathing" and "lying down eases it".
- 90/90 Lift with IR, 90/90 Single Leg Bridge, Hook Plank, Single Leg ISO Heel Bridge Hold: hip-friendly, keep.
- 90/90 Knee Rolls (supine knees side to side): gentle rotation; keep small and pain-free while the hip is irritable.
- Single Leg ISO Hold: likely a single-leg balance/split isometric — confirm the position; lunge-type isometrics
  should be modified while the hip is irritable.

## Hip conflicts elsewhere in the program (for building it into the app later)
Dumbbell Reverse Lunge, DB Cossack Lunge, Single Leg Wall Stride (lunge/split positions) and the seated-position
"90/90" sprint/jump starts would need the go-easy-on-hip handling while the hip is irritable.
