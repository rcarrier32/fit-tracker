# Review of the user's moves: where each one belongs

2026-11-11. Every move from the user's **Hip Mobility** and **Low Back Body** notes, his saved reels
(read from screen recordings) and the PJF / Proformance recordings, judged on what it trains and how
well the evidence supports it for an almost-40 man who coaches a lot and sits at a computer. Evidence
and doses come from [the daily mobility report](../Daily%20hip%20and%20body%20health%20at%2040.md)
and its notes; no new sources. Where a move's identity is uncertain, see the "Stand-ins" table in
[facebook_reels.md](facebook_reels.md).

## Rules used

1. **Hip rotation (internal/external) is the range most linked to hip and groin trouble** in athletes
   (Tak 2018): rotation work earns a daily slot.
2. **Desk sitting costs hip extension** (about 6° less in heavy sitters, cross-sectional) and a short
   hip-flexor stretching block raises it: extension work earns a daily slot.
3. **Range of motion follows weekly stretching time**, about 5-10 min per area per week, and fades
   within weeks without it: static and end-range holds belong in the daily routine.
4. **Long static holds (60 s+) right before lifting or play cost about 4-7% strength/power**; under
   60 s, about 1-2%: long holds go **after** training or in the daily routine, not in a pre-lift warm-up.
   Pre-training warm-ups use short, dynamic moves.
5. **Balance training has the strongest injury-prevention evidence** (ankle sprains −35-46%): daily.
6. **Strength and tendon work needs load 2-3x a week**, not light daily reps: loaded single-leg and
   step-down work belongs in the training plan.
7. Strength of evidence for joint-distraction ("band pulls the joint apart") claims is low: option only.

## What the app does with this

- **Daily C** = 90/90 Hip Opener, Optional Pigeon Length Under Load, Knee Pull Heel Bridge, Hip Flexor
  Length Under Load, Lateral Lunge Length Under Load, Hamstring Floss, Single-Leg Balance (after the
  low-back opener). Daily A and B already carry 90/90 and balance.
- Options sit in each move's Swap list ("Plan alternatives").
- Tags decide the training side: dynamic moves have `role: warmup` (eligible for generated warm-ups,
  no preference over other moves); held moves have `role: cooldown` (eligible for generated
  cooldowns). Knee Pull Heel Bridge was retagged from warm-up to cooldown in this review.
