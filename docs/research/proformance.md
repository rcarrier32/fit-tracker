Researched 2026-11-21 for the hip plan (left hip scope, labral repair + FAI, 7 months out; outer-hip / gluteal
tendon symptoms; pickup basketball; "fix, not avoid").
**In the app since v205 (2026-11-21, the user's "all"):** the ten best moves plus Standing Bench Pigeon Hinge and
Isometric Wall Sit, as tagged coach moves (`data/build_coach_moves.py`, source `proformance`), each playing her YouTube
short from the second the move starts. Where they went: Hip Plan Session A (Bench IR Knee Taps, optional; Standing Bench
Pigeon Hinge; Hip Flexor + IR Combo), Session B (Two-Bench Side Plank with Top-Leg Abduction; Quadruped Abduction
Lift-Overs; Cossack Squat), Daily A (Squat Knee Taps), Daily C (Seated Straight-Leg Raise with Rotation; Quadruped
Abduction Lift-Overs), Basketball Warm-up (Cossack Switches); swaps: Squat Shifts, SI-Joint Band-Traction Side-Lying
Rotations and Weighted Standing Side Bend (low-back reset), Cossack Squat (warm-up, Daily C adductors); Isometric Wall
Sit is catalog-only. Seated Leg Hurdle Lift-Overs now plays her YouTube short. The rest below is still just research. Her
YouTube shorts play inside the app, so the **YouTube id** column is the video to use; the Facebook reel is the
fallback.

## Who she is

- **Dr. Alyssa Lu, DPT**, physical therapist, brand **Proformance** (Proformance Sports Therapy,
  proformancesportspt.com, a "PROFORMANCE" app with paid programs: Beginner Hip Mobility, 8-Week Beginner
  Mobility, Office Detox). Mid-30s and says so often ("almost 40").
- **YouTube** [@proformancesportstherapy](https://www.youtube.com/@proformancesportstherapy): 396 uploads, all
  embeddable; 370 are 6–60 s shorts, 26 are 3–10 min follow-alongs. By title/description about 180 touch hips,
  85 low back/SI, 71 shoulders/posture, 24 squat, 18 knee, 16 ankle/foot, 11 wrist/neck.
- **Facebook** ([reels tab](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100083361331384&sk=reels_tab)): far more than
  140 reels. The tab kept loading past **720** tiles; 247 ids with views were captured (appendix). FB captions are
  the same text as the YouTube descriptions (same posts, cross-posted), so FB reels were matched to shorts by
  caption + length.
- **Her style:** "stretch, then own the range, then load it." Seated straddle hip-flexor lift-overs, Cossack
  progressions, IR drills with bands and a foam roller, a two-bench side plank for the glute med, deep-squat
  "live in the bottom" work, SI-joint band traction. On-screen labels name most moves; captions are mostly
  motivational and rarely list the moves.
